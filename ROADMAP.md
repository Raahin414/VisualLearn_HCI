# VisualLearn roadmap

This is a staged roadmap, not a promise to conduct eight human studies. Follow the original specification and latest user scope. No recruitment before approved protocol, consent, content and infrastructure validation.

| Milestone | Completion evidence | Status / dependencies |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Local foundation improvements | Canonical lesson, accessible media, resilient assessments, isolated synthetic demo and passing checks | Current milestone; questionnaire drafts and local PostgreSQL concurrency are next refinements |
| 2. Multimedia learning experiments | E2 modality/E3 chunking strategies and separately reviewed pilot contracts | Deferred until E1 engineering validity; E4–E8 disabled |
| 3. Additional courses and educational content | Reuse canonical schema, original licensed assets and objective mapping | Deferred until Water Cycle E1 is validated; none in this session |
| 4. Research experiment and assessment completion | Reviewed parallel forms, ordering/labeling/scenarios, blind ratings, debrief and distinct optional seven-day path | Partial; needs supervisor/educator decisions and registered measures |
| 5. Research dashboard and data analysis | Codebook, item exports, quality/retention views, reproducible estimates | Protected exports and scaffold exist; complete locally with labeled fixtures first |
| 6. Supabase staging and research validation | Authorized staging, non-owner role, two-client allocation, connected recovery/timing/export/security checks | Explicitly deferred for this session; not required for demos |
| 7. Deployment and final evaluation | HTTPS/backups/retention/MFA, approved content/consent freeze, staged pilot and responsible reporting | Blocked by earlier milestones/institutional approvals |

Stabilize E1 before adding modalities or courses. Finish a bounded milestone, document actual checks and push without force or unrelated history changes.
