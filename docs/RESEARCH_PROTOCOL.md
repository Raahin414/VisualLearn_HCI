# Draft protocol snapshot

**Not approved. No recruitment authorized.** Based on the supplied v2 specification dated 2026-10-16.

E1 is a between-participant study of three visual-density packages. Canonical factual content, audio, objectives and essential controls stay constant. Randomize within age stratum after baseline, using least-filled arms with secure random tie-breaking under a PostgreSQL transaction/advisory lock. Retain withdrawn assignments in allocation counts.

Timeline: F0 consent/background → A baseline → assigned five-segment lesson → B immediate + free recall → F3 exploratory questionnaire → C at server lesson completion +60 minutes + free recall → F5 review/exposure survey → completion. Draft window closes +90 minutes. Late attempts are rejected, not assigned zero or silently substituted. Seven-day hooks are reserved but disabled.

Draft primary endpoint: adjusted immediate objective percentage. Confirm one endpoint before collection. Eight objective items per form and five-criterion 0–2 recall rubric need expert review. Current MCQs are not proven parallel forms and richer ordering/labeling and scenario rubric tasks remain incomplete. F3/F5 are study-designed drafts, not validated scales. No composite engagement or cognitive load score is calculated.

Population, sample size/power, age bands, instruments, accessible accommodations, viewport policy, language support, final time window/late policy, ethics, consent contact, audio redistribution and debrief must be approved. Minor enrollment is blocked. E2–E8 are disabled roadmap definitions; they are separate experiments, not crossed factors in E1.
